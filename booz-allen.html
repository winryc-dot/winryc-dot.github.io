SYSTEMS ENGINEERING INTERN · BOOZ ALLEN HAMILTON

Digital twin & SLAM testing

June 2026 – August 2026

During my internship at Booz Allen Hamilton, I worked with a multidisciplinary team to develop an autonomous drone swarming simulation using NVIDIA Isaac Sim. I supported the creation of a virtual testing environment by importing and rendering a digital twin in Blender using Google 3D Tiles. I also used Python and AI-assisted tools to execute, test, and evaluate a SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) program to support autonomous drone navigation. Through this experience, I gained hands-on exposure to systems engineering, simulation, digital twins, autonomous systems, and collaborative technical development.
